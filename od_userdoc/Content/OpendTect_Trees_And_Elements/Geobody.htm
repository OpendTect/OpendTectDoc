3.12 Geobody

Geobodies are displayed and created from this tree item. Using the option "New polygon based Geobody" the bodies can be drawn by picking on vertical and horizontal slices. The Geobody will always be the convex envelope around the picked locations.

It is also possible to create Geobodies from:

  • An isovalue surfaceisovalue surface (implicit representation): The Geobody is extracted from a volume based on the amplitude distribution. (See: 3.3 Volume)
  • A polygonpolygon projected between two horizons.

Geobodies may be used for display but also the creation of volumes using the volume buildervolume builder: The inner and/or outer parts of the Geobody are filled with constant value(s).
