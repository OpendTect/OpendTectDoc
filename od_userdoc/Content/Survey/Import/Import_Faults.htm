4.3.4 Import Faults

3D Faults (planes) can be imported in OpendTect via Survey -> Import -> Faults, from Ascii files.

Faults may be used as display element in the 3D scene, displayed in full or in section.

Attributes can also be applied along faults when they are displayed in full.

Faults may be edited by displaying their fault sticks and then repositioning their component nodes.

They may be imported as single faults or in bulk.
