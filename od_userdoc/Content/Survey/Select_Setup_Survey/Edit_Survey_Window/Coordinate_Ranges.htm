4.1.3.2 Coordinate Settings

The relationship between inline/crossline and X/Y can be specified in two ways.

The easy way is to use this tab to specify three points, two of which must be on the same inline. The fourth point will be calculated automatically. Due to rounding off errors, this method may not be 100% accurate.

The alternative method, which is more accurate, is outlined in the next sub-topic: I/C to X/Y Transformation
